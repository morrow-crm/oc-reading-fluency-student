Welcome

This page helps you read a passage aloud for your composition class. Here is what you will do:

  1. Test your microphone.
  2. Load the reading file your professor gave you.
  3. Read the passage aloud. The page will time you and record your voice while you read.
  4. Lightly mark up the reading: tap words you don't know, and tag passages that are confusing, that raise questions, or that you like.
  5. Answer a few short reflection questions.
  6. Download two files (the recording and a reading record) and turn them in to your professor.
You will need a working microphone and a quiet space. Nothing you do here is uploaded or saved on a server — your work lives only in this browser tab until you download it.

Microphone check

Before you read, let's make sure your microphone works. Record about ten seconds of yourself speaking, then listen to the playback.
