## 2026-09-08 — AAC&U (Institute team) — Final reminders for the Institute Kick-Off

**Received:** Sep 8, 2026 · **From:** AAC&U Institute on AI, Pedagogy, and the Curriculum (no-reply)
**Direct inquiries to:** Hannah Schneider, hschneider@aacu.org
**Attachment:** AI Institute Action Plan Template 2026-27 (.docx) —
saved to [`../00-source/AI_Institute_Action_Plan_Template_2627.docx`](../00-source/AI_Institute_Action_Plan_Template_2627.docx)

> Dear Institute on AI, Pedagogy, and the Curriculum Participants,
>
> We are looking forward to the Kick-Off, which starts in two days (September 10 & 11, 2026
> from 11am-5:30pm ET on both days). Below are our final reminders for the Institute Kick-Off.
>
> **Full Program**
>
> The full program is available here: https://aacu.box.com/s/0ml398ux08cwpmzkjgtzbus42js3ltce.
> The program contains all concurrent sessions as well as faculty mentor assignments and
> individual meeting times for Thursday and Friday (pages 35-42).
>
> **Zoom Events Platform Access**
>
> You should have received an email from our Zoom Events platform with the subject line
> "Access the 2026-2027 Institute on AI, Pedagogy, and the Curriculum Kick-Off" from
> 2026-27 AAC&U IAIPC. Please read this email carefully as it contains important information
> about accessing live sessions. As a reminder, all concurrent and plenary sessions will be
> recorded for viewing throughout the year. Advising sessions and action plan presentations
> will NOT be recorded.
>
> You MUST use the unique link in this email/calendar invitation to access the sessions on
> Thursday and Friday. If you do not have your link, please email hschneider@aacu.org and
> akleppin@aacu.org as soon as possible.
>
> If you and your team are able to gather in person, you are welcome to use one participant
> link to join advising sessions.
>
> **Platform Walkthrough Recording**
>
> Please review the platform walkthrough, which was a live session we held last Friday:
> https://aacu.zoom.us/rec/share/BmDzJPYgI25JDrkKY0ZhTJSkPlT7kHJrAlVFEQfnZOoJLI6hN8D8jio83KrzSb88.sqRqwPT1p8LPdl7h.
> This recording shows tips and tricks for navigating our live platform.
>
> **AI Notetaker Statement**
>
> In previous AI Institutes, some participants shared that the presence of AI notetakers in
> Zoom meeting sessions diminished their comfort-level and prevented them from making the
> decision to speak openly and candidly about their challenges and goals. As a result, we will
> not be permitting Zoom-based AI Notetakers within concurrent sessions, advising sessions, or
> action plan presentations. This reflects a desire to create a welcoming culture of openness,
> collegiality, and community for everyone that we hope carries through the entire Institute
> Kick-Off and beyond. If you have any questions or would like to discuss this decision in
> greater length, please contact Eddie Watson (watson@aacu.org).
>
> Please don't hesitate to reach out with any questions. We're looking forward to working with
> all of you.
>
> Please do not reply to this email. Direct all inquiries to Hannah Schneider.
