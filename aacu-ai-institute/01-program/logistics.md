# Logistics, Platforms & Policies

## Two platforms

**Institute Hub** — https://www.aacu.org/institutes/2026-27-iaipc-hub
Session recordings, handouts, articles, Action Plan template, team directory, faculty bios.

- Username: the email address on your AAC&U account (almost always your institutional email).
- Forgot the password? Use "Reset Your Password" on that page.
- Key menus: **Tools & Materials → Resources** (Action Plan template, one-page statements),
  **Schedule/Recordings** (group Zoom access link), **Faculty Mentors** (bios and expertise),
  **Team Directory** (other teams' emails).

**Zoom Events** — all synchronous sessions: Kick-Off, Mid-Institute Event, Capstone, and the
fall & spring webinars.

- Before each event every participant gets a **unique access link** by email from
  `no-reply@zoom.us`, subject line "Access the 2026-2027 Institute on AI, Pedagogy, and the
  Curriculum Kick-Off," sender "2026-27 AAC&U IAIPC."
- **You must use that unique link** to get into Thursday and Friday sessions. One link covers
  every session in the event.
- A group access link also lives on the Hub under "Schedule/Recordings" as a fallback.
- Missing your link? Email **hschneider@aacu.org and akleppin@aacu.org** immediately.
- If the team gathers in person, one participant's link is enough to join advising sessions.

## AI notetaker policy

**Zoom-based AI notetakers are not permitted** in concurrent sessions, advising sessions, or
Action Plan presentations. Participants in previous institutes said notetakers made them less
willing to speak candidly about challenges. Questions: Eddie Watson, watson@aacu.org.

Practical consequence for us: someone on the team takes notes by hand. Use
[`../04-notes/_TEMPLATE-session-notes.md`](../04-notes/_TEMPLATE-session-notes.md).
