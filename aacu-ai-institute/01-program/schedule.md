## Structure

Time is split roughly two ways: **concurrent sessions** (four bands total) and
**team time / consulting**. All sessions are discussion-based and interactive.
Recordings of all presentations post to the Institute Hub afterward.

Advising: mentors meet their teams as a **group** Thursday 1:00–1:30 pm ET, then
**individually** in the 30-minute slots above. Any Institute faculty member — not just your
assigned mentor — can be asked for an additional 30-minute consultation. There's no formal
scheduling process; email them directly (see [faculty-directory.md](faculty-directory.md)).
