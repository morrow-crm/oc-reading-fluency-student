## How to use this directory

1. **Before a session** — skim [sessions.md](01-program/sessions.md), pick a block. The team should
   divide and conquer across the four concurrent bands rather than all attending the same thing.
2. **During a session** — copy `04-notes/_TEMPLATE-session-notes.md` to
   `04-notes/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` and take notes there.
3. **After a session** — pull anything actionable into the "Candidate commitments" section of
   [action-plan-draft.md](03-deliverables/action-plan-draft.md).
4. **Deliverables** — drafts go in `03-deliverables/`. Keep a dated final copy when submitted.
5. **Anything the team produces** — syllabus, policy draft, workshop deck, student handout —
   gets a row in the artifact table in
   [final-report-draft.md](03-deliverables/final-report-draft.md) the day it exists. The Final
   Report asks for these, and reconstructing them in April is miserable.
6. **Email from the Institute** — paste it into the chat and it gets appended to
   [05-correspondence/correspondence.md](05-correspondence/correspondence.md), with action
   items and deadlines pulled forward into this README.

## Notes on the program's stated goals

The curriculum was built around goals teams named in their applications. Ranked by how many
teams cited them:

- AI literacy and curriculum integration — 91.8%
- Campus-wide AI strategy or institutional framework — 86.6%
- Teaching, pedagogy, assessment, and course design — 85.8%
- AI governance, policy, ethics, and responsible use — 82.1%
- Faculty development and professional learning — 65.7%
- Identifying, evaluating, and implementing AI tools — 56.0%
- Student AI competencies and workforce readiness — 50.7%

Also named: data security/privacy/cybersecurity, administrative uses of AI, operational
efficiency, and building communities of practice across campus.
