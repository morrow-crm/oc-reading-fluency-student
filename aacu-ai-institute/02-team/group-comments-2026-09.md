## What these imply for the plan

**The 2–3 year vision is broader than faculty development.** Five of the eight comments describe
an end state that includes institutional AI policy covering use, tools, and privacy — across
instruction *and* the rest of campus — plus a clear statement of what tools are approved at the
institutional level, and a named, prioritized, and scheduled set of policies and procedures.
Faculty development and AI literacy/pedagogy sit inside that larger picture.

This is compatible with the team's decision to focus on faculty development, but the plan has to
say so explicitly: the vision is institution-wide; the lever we are pulling this year is faculty
development; the AI literacy definition is the shared vocabulary that later policy work depends
on. Otherwise a reader sees a vision the project doesn't deliver.

**"Schedule of offerings, frequency of offerings and joining"** is a concrete specification for
the PD plan — not just what gets taught but a published calendar, a stated cadence, and a clear
way to sign up. Worth writing into the plan in those terms.

**The survey is student/staff-based, per comment 7.** The team described it as faculty survey
data from Spring 2026. Either there are two instruments or the scope is wider than "faculty."
Needs resolving — it changes what Step 1 can actually conclude.

**Academic dishonesty metrics** (comment 8) are proposed as a success measure tied to the survey.
This is a real fork: integrity metrics are attractive to administrators and easy to misread —
referrals can rise because reporting improved, not because behavior worsened. If we use them,
pair them with a measure of expectation-setting so the number can be read honestly.
